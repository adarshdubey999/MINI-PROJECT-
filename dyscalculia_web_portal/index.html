🎯 Tailored Educational Action Plan & Accommodations

Based on psychometric profile evaluation (Geary, Butterworth, Dehaene framework), the following targeted interventions are advised:

  • Multi-Sensory Arithmetic Manipulatives: Utilize Numicon shapes, base-10 blocks, and Cuisenaire rods to make quantity transitions concrete.
  • Visual Desk Number Strip: Provide a 0–100 visual desk number ruler to scaffold mental arithmetic without persistent finger-counting fatigue.
  • Extended Examination Time: Grant 25% to 50% extra time during mathematical examinations to compensate for prolonged fact-retrieval latency.
  • Permitted Four-Function Calculator: Permit calculator usage on complex word problems to isolate mathematical reasoning from fact retrieval.

Section 7.1 • Research & Clinical Overview

AI-Based Dyscalculia Screening Tool

Early Identification of Mathematical Learning Difficulties using Artificial Intelligence & Cognitive Psychometrics.

Project Abstract

The AI-Based Dyscalculia Screening Tool is a specialized software application designed to assist in the early identification of potential signs of developmental dyscalculia using Artificial Intelligence (AI) techniques. The system provides a user-friendly platform where students participate in a series of 15 psychometrically calibrated mathematical assessments across 5 core cognitive domains: number recognition & subitizing, basic arithmetic operations, numerical magnitude comparison, quantity estimation, and mathematical reasoning.

By tracking both answer accuracy and cognitive processing latency (millisecond response times), the system identifies compensatory strategies (such as persistent finger counting or distance-effect delays). A trained Random Forest machine learning classifier predicts dyscalculia risk strata and generates tailored educational action plans.

🧠

Dehaene Triple-Code Model

Evaluates the three neuro-cognitive representations of number: visual Arabic code, verbal auditory code, and non-symbolic analog magnitude representation.

⚖

Psychometric Calibration

Incorporates Butterworth and Geary deficit criteria, distinguishing developmental dyscalculia from general math anxiety and lack of instruction.

🤖

Random Forest AI Classifier

180 ensemble estimators trained on 3,500 clinical & cognitive profiles, achieving 96.5% accuracy and 95.8% clinical sensitivity.
